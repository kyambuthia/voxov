## 3. Reasoned Conclusions

1. **Terrain quality:** Decent height variation with a noisy, natural‑looking distribution. Block faces are mostly solid, but there are noticeable gaps/black areas that break the terrain continuity.
2. **Rendering bugs:** The primary issue is the presence of black gaps/holes, especially along the right side and at the edges of elevated columns. These are likely caused by:
   - Incorrect face culling at chunk boundaries (neighboring chunk faces not being generated)
   - Missing geometry for certain block faces
   - Potential z‑fighting or depth‑buffer issues.
3. **Lighting:** Extremely basic – flat, uniform colors per material with no apparent directional lighting, ambient occlusion, or shadows. The scene looks "unlit."
4. **Overall visual quality:** **4/10** – The core voxel terrain is rendered and recognizable, but the black gaps, lack of lighting, and absence of textures or shading significantly reduce visual polish. It’s a functional prototype but needs work on mesh generation (filling gaps) and lighting.

## 4. Recommended Next Action

1. **Fix mesh generation:** Investigate why black gaps appear. Most likely, faces between chunks are not being generated when neighboring chunks are missing or not loaded. Implement a system to generate faces at chunk borders based on the presence/absence of neighboring blocks (even across chunk boundaries).
2. **Add basic lighting:** Implement a simple directional light (sun) with per‑face shading (diffuse + ambient) to give depth and form to the terrain.
3. **Verify face culling:** Ensure that internal faces between solid blocks are culled, but external faces (exposed to air) are always generated, regardless of chunk loading state.
4. **Debug the black areas:** Add a debug view to highlight missing faces or check chunk neighbor queries.

**Suggested immediate step:** Run the game with the developer HUD (F2) to see `streamed_chunk_count` and other metrics. Check if the black areas correlate with unloaded or partially loaded chunks. Then review the mesh‑building code (likely in `block_world_.build_chunk_mesh()`) to ensure it generates faces at chunk boundaries correctly.
